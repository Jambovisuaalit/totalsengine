05 / COMMON QUESTIONS

The important
fine print.

Will there be a pick every day?

No. This is designed to be selective. Some slates may have no qualifying opportunities at all.

Does a positive CLV guarantee profits?

No. CLV can be a useful indicator of price quality when measured consistently, but it does not guarantee profitability or eliminate model risk and variance.

How are injury reports accounted for?

Source reports are timestamped and normalized. Missing or uncertain player-impact estimates should trigger caution, not invented certainty.

Does the analysis include overtime?

The market definition must explicitly match the bookmaker's NBA full-game totals rules, including overtime where applicable. Different rules are not mixed.

Can I subscribe now?

Not yet. Subscription payments and Telegram access remain inactive pending operational validation and confirmation of payment-provider eligibility.

Is NBA Totals Engine affiliated with the NBA?

No. This is an independent sports-data research project, not endorsed by or affiliated with the NBA or its teams.
