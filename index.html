STILL CURIOUS?

Good questions.
Straight answers.

The details worth knowing before considering any sports research subscription.

Is the service available to subscribe to now?

No. Enrollment and payment processing are disabled until the technical checks and payment-provider eligibility are confirmed.

Will I receive a bet every day?

No. The intended output is selective. If the data, price or model confidence is inadequate, the correct decision can be to pass.

Is the analysis guaranteed to make money?

No. All forecasts have uncertainty, and positive closing-line value does not guarantee profit. The public performance record has not yet been verified.

What data does the model consider?

The planned research pipeline includes NBA game and play-by-play data, bookmaker totals history and player-injury reports. Data availability and licensing can limit coverage.

Does the total include overtime?

Each comparison must use the same full-game totals settlement rules as the bookmaker, including overtime where applicable. Incompatible market rules are rejected.

Is Totals Engine affiliated with the NBA?

No. NBA Totals Engine is an independent analytics project. It is not associated with or endorsed by the NBA or any team.
