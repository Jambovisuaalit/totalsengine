STILL CURIOUS?

Good questions.
Straight answers.

What this sports analytics prototype illustrates, and what remains unverified.

Is this a live service or a paid product?

No. This is a non-commercial personal research and interface demonstration. It does not offer subscriptions, take payments, collect signups or provide betting tips.

Will I receive a bet every day?

No. The intended output is selective. If the data, price or model confidence is inadequate, the correct decision can be to pass.

Is the analysis guaranteed to make money?

No. All forecasts have uncertainty, and positive closing-line value does not guarantee profit. The public performance record has not yet been verified.

What data does the model consider?

The current frozen rolling-baseline model uses historical completed NBA game totals. Play-by-play, injury and bookmaker feeds are not verified inputs to this forward model.

Does the total include overtime?

The research study compares predicted full-game totals with final combined NBA points, including overtime. No bookmaker settlement or executable odds are currently evaluated.

Is Totals Engine affiliated with the NBA?

No. NBA Totals Engine is an independent analytics project. It is not associated with or endorsed by the NBA or any team.
